## 📘 Overview
This document defines the governance model and operational processes that sustain the Coeurl Community.  
It covers mentorship programs, contributor onboarding, promotion workflows, and internal documentation practices that ensure consistency, accountability, and adherence to established norms.  
These procedures promote transparency, fair evaluation, and structured delegation of responsibility across thousands of community participants.

---

## 🧭 Mentorship Program

### Purpose
The **Mentorship Program** provides a structured onboarding path for new contributors.  
Mentors guide new members through operational expectations, communication etiquette, and practical procedures for contributing to community activities.

### Key Components
- **Mentors:** Experienced contributors responsible for ensuring new members learn and adhere to operational norms.  
- **Trials:** New contributors undergoing an evaluation period before gaining full permissions.  
- **Documentation:** Each mentor follows the standardized *Mentor Guide* which includes:
  - Purpose and Responsibilities  
  - Communication Guidelines  
  - Channel References  
  - Trial Process Overview  

### Trial Process
1. Mentor introduces the trial contributor to their relevant team.  
2. Contributor observes and participates to learn established workflows.  
3. Mentor reviews live activities (e.g., Faloop reporting or event management).  
4. Performance and community cohesion are reviewed before final promotion.

## 🪜 Contributor Promotions & Demotions

### Promotion Criteria
Promotions follow documented guidelines within the *Faloop Guidelines for the Primal Datacenter*.  
Eligibility is based on demonstrated reliability, activity level, and adherence to conduct standards.  

| Promotion Type | Description | Criteria |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| **B-Science Promotion** | Grants the ability to post or confirm “B-Rank” hunt reports. | Minimum reliability threshold and mentor endorsement. |
| **Instant Posting Promotion** | Enables contributors to post hunt spawns instantly. | Proven judgment and adherence to community timing standards. |
| **Early Access (EA) Promotion** | Extends elevated permissions to manage manage and queue spawns. | Requires extended tenure and clean history. |

### Demotion Policy
Demotions may occur for inactivity, failure to comply with operational standards, or conduct violations.  
The policy emphasizes education and improvement over punishment. Documentation is recorded in private moderation logs.

## 📜 Code of Conduct & Expectations

### Behavioral Standards
- Maintain professionalism and respect across all community spaces.  
- Refrain from disruptive behavior, misinformation, or hostile interactions.  
- Collaborate constructively and avoid interfering with other contributors’ work.  

### Enforcement
Moderators enforce rules through escalating responses:
1. Verbal or written warnings.  
2. Temporary communication restrictions.  
3. Removal of contributor privileges.  
4. Permanent ban (admin review required).  

All actions are logged in the moderation archives for accountability.
