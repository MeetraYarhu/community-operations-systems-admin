# 🎓 Coeurl Mentor Guide  
**Project:** Coeurl Discord — Faloop Mentorship Framework  
**Role:** Community Administrator / Documentation Author  
**Duration:** 2021 – Present  
**Purpose:** Outlines responsibilities, communication standards, and the structured trial process for mentors supporting new Faloop spawners across the Primal Data Center.


---

## 🧭 Overview

The **Faloop Mentor Program** oversees the onboarding and evaluation of new trial spawners within the Primal DC.  
Mentors ensure that new participants receive fair, consistent treatment, adhere to Faloop’s rules, and understand the standards expected of all contributors.

This guide outlines Mentor responsibilities, communication standards, and the trial process used across community operations.

---

## ⚙️ Purpose & Responsibilities

Mentors serve as representatives of the Tempo-Sapphire community and play a key role in ensuring process consistency across all worlds.

### Core Responsibilities
- Identify new potential trials and prevent eligible spawners from being overlooked.  
- Ensure trials experience a **fair, standard, and transparent** evaluation process.  
- Maintain alignment between worlds to minimize cross-world friction or uneven standards.  
- Communicate a **neutral, standardized** set of expectations to all hunters, regardless of local preferences.  
- Remain active and integrated in both local spawn and train circles.  
- Identify and report:
  - Positive interactions (e.g., successful spawns or collaboration)
  - Potential concerns (e.g., sonar use, etiquette issues, falsified reports)
- Coordinate with other mentors and moderators to develop action plans for any issues.
- Keep mentor discussions and trial information **confidential**.
- Represent the Primal hunt community professionally in public spaces.

> **Note:**  
> Mentors are expected to remain friendly and approachable. Personal opinions are fine in private spaces, but in public Discord and in-game channels, conduct should reflect positively on the broader community.

---

## 📋 General Guidelines to Communicate with Trials

Mentors are responsible for ensuring trials understand and comply with the community’s operational guidelines.  
While `#faloop-guidelines` provides a full reference, the following highlights are essential to reinforce:

- 🚫 **No use of memory-reading or relinking tools** (e.g., Sonar, HuntRadar).  
- ⏳ **No Party Finder listings** before a mark is officially released.  
- 🔒 **Do not relay scheduled spawns** before release or share restricted science data.  
- 🕐 **Pinged ≠ auto-release.** Confirm release order in `#spawner-lounge`.  
- ⚠️ **Discourage post-maint rushes.** Encourage coordinated link chaining to prevent chaos.  
- 👁️ **Stay with your spawn** unless another mentor confirms coverage.  
- 📡 Ensure marks are properly recorded in Early Access (EA) and released in queue order.  
- 📆 Maintain awareness of spawn timers and death records.

## 🧱 Trial Process for Mentors

Mentors guide the entire lifecycle of a trial — from identification to promotion.

### 1. Identify Potential Spawners
Potential candidates may surface through community discussion, CWLs, or observation in `#faloop-discussion`.  
Mentors should approach new spawners respectfully and confirm their interest in participating.

> Our goal is inclusion, not obligation.  
> Some players may not wish to join Faloop systems, and that choice should always be respected.

---

### 2. Initial Conversation
- Explain the Faloop system and expectations in clear, welcoming terms.  
- Reiterate the **zero-tolerance policy** for memory-reading tools (Sonar, etc.).  
- Confirm they actively **spawn** rather than only relay or ping-check.  
- Gauge their understanding and willingness before recommending them for a trial.

---

### 3. Opening a Trial Thread
Once a candidate seems ready:
- Bring them up in `#mentor-chat`.  
- A moderator will create a thread in `#promotion-forums`.  
- Use the thread to share any relevant context—positive interactions, potential concerns, or neutral observations.

Threads generally remain open for **at least 6 hours**, longer if discussion is ongoing.  
This ensures all mentors and moderators have time to contribute.

---

### 4. Integration and Monitoring
- Once promoted to **Trial** in Faloop Admin, ensure they join a CWL or receive onboarding support.  
- Provide them a walkthrough of Faloop posting and release flow.  
- Stay in contact during their trial period to ensure fairness and answer questions.  
- Check in regularly—mentorship should feel **supportive, not evaluative**.

---

### 5. Promotion to Early Access (EA)
- Trials are typically promoted to **EA** after ~1 month of active, positive participation.  
- Inactivity or unresolved issues may extend this period.  
- If a trial remains active for much longer than expected, alert a moderator to review.  
- Moderators manage thread creation for **Trial → EA** transitions.
