# 🗺️ Faloop Information  
**Project:** Coeurl Discord — Faloop Integration Overview  
**Role:** Community Administrator / Documentation Author  
**Duration:** 2021 – Present  
**Purpose:** Public-facing overview explaining what Faloop is, how it integrates with the Coeurl Discord, and how community members engage with its systems.


---

## 🧭 Overview

**Faloop** is an external platform that aggregates and shares real-time hunt data for *Final Fantasy XIV*. It allows players to track spawn conditions, report sightings, and coordinate large-scale community hunts efficiently.

Within the **Coeurl** community, Faloop serves as the central hub for hunt synchronization. It enables contributors to post hunt reports that instantly notify Discord users through dedicated reporting channels and automated map markers.

Tempo-Sapphire integrates with Faloop to streamline coordination, enhance transparency, and maintain consistent reporting standards across all contributors.

---

## ⚙️ Integration with the Coeurl Community

Our Discord infrastructure connects directly to Faloop’s public data endpoints via authorized Reporter accounts. These Reporter roles are granted to trusted contributors who post verified hunt data.

### Core Integration Features

- **Real-time Map Linking:**  
  Verified reports in Faloop automatically sync to corresponding Discord channels.
- **Automated Announcements:**  
  System posts updates for A-Rank and S-Rank sightings to maintain consistent visibility.
- **Access Control:**  
  Only authorized Reporters can push live data to the system.
- **Fail-safe Posting:**  
  Dual verification ensures reports are valid before appearing in hunt channels.

### Faloop Roles in Coeurl

| Role | Access | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| `Reporter` | Limited | Can post verified sightings to Faloop and Discord. |
| `B-Science` | Intermediate | Can post A/B-Rank data to help calibrate region visibility and scout accuracy. |
| `Instant Posting` | Advanced | Instantly post data. |
| `Early Access` | Extended | Receives posts ahead of public release to assist with preparation. |

## 🧱 Reporter Eligibility and Permissions

Contributor promotions to Faloop Reporter are managed through the **Mentor** and **Administration** teams.

### Requirements
- Demonstrated consistency and accuracy in scouting and reporting.
- Familiarity with Faloop’s reporting interface.
- Adherence to Tempo-Sapphire’s internal data integrity policies.
- Successful review and approval by Mentors or senior Reporters.

### Promotion Path
1. **B-Science Access** – Demonstrates consistent activity and accuracy. Gains permissions to mark A/B-Ranks.
2. **Instant-Posting** – Granted posting rights to Faloop-linked channels.
3. **Early Access** – Reserved for long-standing, trusted contributors.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is Faloop?
Faloop is a community-driven tool for tracking and reporting hunt marks in *Final Fantasy XIV*. It consolidates information across servers and regions to help players coordinate efficiently.

### How do I register for Faloop?
1. Visit [https://faloop.app](https://faloop.app).  
2. Log in with your FFXIV character via Lodestone authentication.  
3. Select your world and region.  
4. Join the Coeurl hunt community through our Discord for access to posting privileges.

### Why can’t I post yet?
New members start without posting rights. Posting is reserved for verified Reporters to prevent false alerts. Once you’ve participated in hunts and demonstrated reliability, you may be eligible for promotion.

### What is “B-Science”?
B-Science refers to the data collection process for B-Rank hunts. Contributors with this access help the community gather spawn data used to maintain accuracy and improve early-train routing.
