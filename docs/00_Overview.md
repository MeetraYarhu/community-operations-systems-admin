# Coeurl Community Overview
**Project:** Coeurl Discord - Primal Data Center Hunt Administration  
**Role:** Community Administrator / Documentation Author  
**Duration:** 2021 – Present  
**Purpose:** High-level overview of the Coeurl community’s structure, infrastructure, and documentation system.  
**Last Updated:** October 2025

---

## Overview

The **Coeurl Hunt Community** is a large, organized group operating on the **Primal Data Center** of *Final Fantasy XIV*.  
It coordinates open-world hunt events, manages Faloop integration, and maintains a fair and efficient ecosystem for thousands of hunters.

This documentation repository consolidates all administrative references, moderator and mentor training materials, and contributor guidelines that define how Coeurl operates.

---

## Administrative Structure

| Role | Description |
|------|--------------|
| **Administrators** | Oversee the community’s overall structure, technical integrations, and policy consistency across Primal. |
| **Moderators** | Enforce rules, manage Discord operations, and assist with dispute resolution or escalations. |
| **Mentors** | Guide and evaluate new Faloop trials, ensure fair onboarding, and promote inter-world consistency. |
| **Contributors / Reporters** | Actively participate in scouting, reporting, and maintaining Faloop data accuracy. |

Each role works collaboratively to maintain transparency, consistency, and positive community relations across the Primal DC.

---

## Technical Integration

Coeurl’s infrastructure integrates several systems to support real-time hunt coordination:

- **Faloop** — The primary data platform for tracking and reporting hunt marks.  
- **Tempo-Bot** — Custom automation for Discord moderation, announcements, and contributor tools.  
- **Community Databases** — Privately maintained records of trial progress, contributor history, and feedback.  

All systems are designed to uphold data integrity, minimize human error, and streamline coordination across thousands of simultaneous participants.
